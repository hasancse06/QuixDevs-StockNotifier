## Short announcement

QuixDevs StockNotifier monitors WooCommerce inventory and queues a low-stock email when managed quantity crosses your chosen threshold. Choose WordPress product authors, Dokan, WC Vendors or PublishPress Authors as the recipient system. Customize native email templates, preview recipients and review individual outcomes from private admin screens. Free-provider versions and local checks are documented; premium editions and real inbox delivery remain unverified.
