# Release artwork specifications and exports

Finished editable sources and PNG exports are now in [docs/branding](branding/README.md) and [wordpress-org-assets](../wordpress-org-assets/README.md). The design follows the QuixDevs ProductClock family while using an original StockNotifier inventory/email mark.

- **Palette:** navy `#091827`, teal `#35e2cc`, white `#f4fbff`, muted blue `#b3c8d7`.
- **Company identity:** supplied transparent QuixDevs PNG, preserved byte-for-byte and embedded in banners/social artwork without recoloring or stretching.
- **Icon:** inventory box, descending stock bars and envelope; editable SVG with 128 × 128 and 256 × 256 PNGs.
- **Banners:** 772 × 250 and 1544 × 500, product name, subtitle, tagline, company logo and creator credit.
- **Social preview:** 1280 × 640, product name, subtitle, tagline, product mark and creator credit; opaque PNG under 1 MB.
- **Directory screenshots:** six actual local admin captures in readme order: dashboard, settings, templates, inventory, activity, tools. Test fixtures use synthetic data and intercepted mail.

No marketplace trademark logos, fabricated success screens, ratings, download counts or official endorsement claims are present. Finished presentation assets are kept outside runtime `assets/` and the installable ZIP.
